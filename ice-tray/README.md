# Aesthetic Ice Tray

## What is it?
A consumer product redesign of a standard ice tray, designed with focus on 
aesthetic geometry, clean form, and visual appeal — going beyond functional 
design into intentional product aesthetics.

## Design Focus
- Aesthetic-driven surface geometry and proportions
- Consumer product design sensibility in CAD
- Demonstrates design thinking beyond purely functional parts
